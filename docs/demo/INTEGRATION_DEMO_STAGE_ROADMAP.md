## Meaning of completion

Stage 21 completes the synthetic Integration Demo and its independently verifiable evidence chain. It does not constitute institutional or Production authorization.

Environment promotion remains:

`Local Preview -> Integration Demo -> Non-Production Pilot -> Production`

After Stage 21, a participating entity must make and verify its institutional identity, policy, trust, data, infrastructure, security, privacy, operations, continuity, benchmark, and ownership selections in a Non-Production Pilot. Production requires a separate recorded authorization and change window. Repository defaults remain unconfigured and fail closed throughout.
